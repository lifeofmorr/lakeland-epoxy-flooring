Plant City questions, answered

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Plant City, FL?

A 1-car garage is typically $1,200–$2,000, a 2-car $1,900–$3,400, and a 3-car $2,800–$4,800 for a professionally installed full-flake system. Your free quote prices your exact floor.

Can epoxy handle farm and workshop use?

Yes. A diamond-ground full-flake system with a polyaspartic topcoat resists oil, fuel and solvents, and stands up to mower decks, ATVs and trailer traffic that would destroy paint or a DIY kit within a season.

How long does a Plant City garage install take?

Most residential floors take 1–2 days. Walk on it within about 24 hours; park on it after 3–5 days depending on the system.
