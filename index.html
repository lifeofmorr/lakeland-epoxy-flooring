Questions, answered straight

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Lakeland, FL?

A typical 2-car garage (about 450 sq ft) runs $1,900–$3,400 for a professionally installed full-flake system. The average local project is around $2,500. Final pricing depends on concrete condition, moisture, and system choice.

How long does installation take?

Most garage floors take 1–2 days. Walk on it within about 24 hours; park on it after 3–5 days depending on the system.

Will epoxy hold up in Florida heat and humidity?

Yes — when the slab is ground and moisture-tested first. Moisture in Florida concrete is the #1 cause of peeling, so pros test for it and use a moisture-mitigating primer where needed.

Epoxy vs. polyaspartic — what's the difference?

Epoxy is the durable build coat; polyaspartic is a fast-curing, UV-stable topcoat that won't yellow in Florida sun. Many top systems combine both.
