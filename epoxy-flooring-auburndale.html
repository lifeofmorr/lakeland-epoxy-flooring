Auburndale questions, answered

How much does an epoxy garage floor cost in Auburndale, FL?

A 1-car garage is typically $1,200–$2,000, a 2-car $1,900–$3,400, and a 3-car $2,800–$4,800 for a professionally installed full-flake system. Your free quote prices your exact floor.

Can epoxy handle a working garage — tools, mowers and project cars?

Yes. A diamond-ground, full-flake system with a polyaspartic topcoat resists oil, solvents and dropped tools, and it doesn't lift under hot tires the way paint and DIY kits do.

Do Auburndale installers really finish in 1–2 days?

Most residential garage floors do: grinding, crack repair and moisture testing on day one, then coating, flake and topcoat. Walk on it within about 24 hours; park on it after 3–5 days.
