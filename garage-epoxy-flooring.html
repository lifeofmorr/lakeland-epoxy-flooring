Why full-flake, and why professional

The big-box DIY kits fail for one reason: no surface prep. Professional systems start with diamond grinding so the coating bonds to the concrete itself, not to the dirt and sealer sitting on it.

Diamond-ground prep

The slab is ground, cracks and divots are repaired, and moisture is tested before a drop of coating goes down.

Full flake broadcast

Vinyl flakes are broadcast to rejection into the epoxy base — that's what gives the floor its grip, depth and ability to hide dirt.

UV-stable topcoat

A polyaspartic clear coat seals it against Florida sun, hot tires and chemicals. It won't yellow at the door line.
